Science & Academia

Preprint archives give you the newest work but it has not been refereed, and the two should not be conflated. Turkish academic material is scattered, so harvesters like DergiPark and Harman save real time against searching institution by institution.

30 links

ABD Antarktika Programı

usap.gov

The official portal of the Antarctic research programme, with material on field logistics and station operations — an interesting source on systems that work in extreme conditions.

Free, Academic

Aperta

aperta.ulakbim.gov.tr

TÜBİTAK’s open archive, hosting research data alongside publications. Data sharing is not yet common in Turkey, which makes it notable.

Free, Turkish, Data Science, Academic

Arkhe Projesi

archeprojesi.com

Turkish science and philosophy writing that sits between the shallowness of popular science and the opacity of academic register.

Free, Turkish, Academic

arXiv

arxiv.org

The preprint archive for physics, maths and computer science. Publishing before peer review is why the field’s newest work appears here — and why it needs reading with that caveat.

Free, Academic

Connected Papers

connectedpapers.com

Draws the literature around a paper as a similarity graph with prior and derivative work separated — a fast map when entering an unfamiliar field.

Freemium, Academic

David Rumsey Harita Koleksiyonu

davidrumsey.com

A high-resolution historical map archive that lets you georeference maps onto modern geography — a strong tool for tracking place-name and border change.

Free, Academic, Reference

DergiPark

dergipark.org.tr

The open-access platform for Turkish academic journals, hosting over a thousand titles — the primary source for Turkish literature, not aggregated anywhere else.

Free, Turkish, Academic

Devlet Arşivleri

devletarsivleri.gov.tr

Turkey’s official archive institution and the gateway to catalogues and digital copies of Ottoman and Republican-era documents.

Free, Turkish, Reference

DOI

doi.org

The persistent identifier system for scholarly work. A DOI keeps resolving when a journal site moves — the only solid answer to link rot in citations.

Free, Reference, Academic

Düşünce Deneyleri ve Paradokslar

onculanalitikfelsefe.com

A Turkish compilation of famous thought experiments in philosophy of mind — the Chinese Room, zombies, Mary’s room. The philosophical backdrop to current AI arguments.

Free, Turkish, Academic

Ethnologue

ethnologue.com

The standard catalogue of world languages with systematic speaker counts and vitality ratings. It is also the registration authority for ISO 639-3 language codes.

Paid, Academic, Reference

Etimoloji Türkçe

etimolojiturkce.com

An etymological dictionary of Turkish citing its sources, showing which language each word came from, when, and its earliest attestation. Built on Nişanyan’s work.

Free, Turkish, Reference

Harman

harman.ulakbim.gov.tr

A harvester searching Turkey’s institutional academic archives from one place, replacing the university-by-university hunt for theses and articles.

Free, Turkish, Academic

iNaturalist

inaturalist.org

Share nature observations and get species identified; once the community confirms one, the record becomes research-grade and flows into GBIF. A rare case of a hobby producing real data.

Free, Academic

İÜ Gazete Arşivi

nek.istanbul.edu.tr

Istanbul University’s scanned historical newspaper collection — primary sources for period research, where the details secondary literature skips turn up.

Free, Turkish, Reference

Learn Anything

learn-anything.xyz

A learning platform mapping topics and their connections, making visible the adjacencies a linear curriculum hides.

Free, Academic, Curriculum

Nişanyan Yeradları

nisanyanyeradlari.com

A historical inventory of settlement names in and around Turkey, documenting each change with date and source. Nothing else compiles this systematically.

Free, Turkish, Academic

Open Language Archives (arama)

search.language-archives.org

Searches language resources across scattered institutional archives from one place, particularly useful for material on under-documented and endangered languages.

Free, Academic, Reference

Open Language Archives Community

language-archives.org

A community uniting language resource archives under a shared metadata standard — the infrastructure that makes scattered university collections searchable.

Free, Academic, Reference

Oxford Academic

academic.oup.com

Oxford University Press’s journal platform, one of the major publisher archives. Most content sits behind subscription, with open-access articles separately marked.

Paid, Academic

Semantic Scholar

semanticscholar.org

Analyses citation context to answer whether a paper is being supported or criticised. Google Scholar gives you a count; this gives you a direction.

Free, Academic, RAG

SI Ön Ekleri (NIST)

nist.gov

The official table of unit prefixes, including recent additions like ronna and quetta — the authoritative reference for unit conversion.

Free, Reference

SKYbrary

skybrary.aero

An aviation safety knowledge base of accident analyses, human factors and safety management systems — a usable archive for anyone thinking about high-reliability system design.

Free, Academic, Security

SkyViewer

skyviewer.app

Browses sky survey data by position, letting you overlay telescope imagery and compare across wavelengths.

Free, Academic, Data Science

Stellarium Web

stellarium-web.org

Shows the real sky for your location in the browser, with time you can wind forward to see planetary positions. No installation.

Open Source, Academic, In Browser

Transkribus · Osmanlıca

transkribus.org

A text-recognition model trained on printed Ottoman Turkish. General OCR cannot read Arabic-script Turkish, and that gap is critical in archive research.

Freemium, Turkish, Reference

Vikipedi Portalları

en.wikipedia.org

An index of Wikipedia’s topic portals — a structured way into a field instead of random search, and a way not to enter a new subject without a map.

Free, Reference

Wiki Graph

blinpete.github.io

Explores Wikipedia’s link network as a graph. Seeing conceptual distance without reading the text sketches a field’s structure quickly.

Open Source, Interactive, Academic

Wikimedia Commons

commons.wikimedia.org

An archive of freely usable images and media with the licence stated explicitly on every file, which removes the legal ambiguity when sourcing visuals for a talk or publication.

Free, Reference

Wolfram Alpha

wolframalpha.com

A computational knowledge engine that integrates, converts units and compares data. A search engine finds the answer; this computes it, showing the working step by step in the paid tier.

Freemium, Academic
